    How to play
    • Start: tap any unclaimed land on the world map to place your capital. AI nations can't attack you during spawn protection.
    • Attack: pick how many troops to send (10–75%), then tap land next to your border: empty land, a tribe, or a nation. Your border pushes forward tile by tile until those troops run out.
    • Troops regrow on their own. More land means more troops, up to your troop cap. Gold comes in steadily.
    • Build: tap your own land (or press Build). City raises your troop cap, Factory adds gold, Port lets you send boats across the sea (tap land overseas), Defense Post makes nearby land 3× harder to take, Missile Silo unlocks nukes, SAM shoots down nukes.
    • Nukes wipe out troops and buildings in a radius and leave fallout. Fallout doesn't count toward the goal, and anyone can reclaim it.
    • Chem Strike (cheaper, also from a Missile Silo) releases a drifting gas cloud. For about 25 seconds it drains troops from whoever owns the land underneath and stops their troop growth there. Buildings survive. SAM sites can shoot it down too.
    • Alliances: nations may offer you peace. Long-press (or right-click) a nation to see its info or ask for an alliance.
    • Win by owning 80% of the world. You lose if your territory is wiped out.
    • Drag to pan, pinch or scroll to zoom.
